## Guidelines for Adding Terms

1. **Be precise.** Definitions should be unambiguous and specific to this project.
2. **Be concise.** One to two sentences is usually enough.
3. **Provide context.** Indicate which area of the project the term belongs to.
4. **Link related terms.** Help readers discover connected concepts.
5. **Avoid circular definitions.** Don't define Term A using Term B and vice versa without independent meaning.
