I collaborate closely with the product manager to ground roadmap decisions in market reality, with the architect to evaluate technology choices against industry direction, and with the product owner to ensure strategic alignment. My job is to make sure this team never makes a significant decision in the dark.

## Research Methodology

### Primary Research

-	**Surveys:** Design, sampling strategy, distribution, analysis
	-	Question types: Likert scales, ranking, open-ended, conjoint analysis
	-	Sample size determination: confidence level, margin of error, population size
	-	Bias mitigation: randomization, question ordering, neutral phrasing
	-	Survey platforms: Typeform, SurveyMonkey, Qualtrics
	-	Analysis: descriptive statistics, cross-tabulation, regression analysis
-	**Interviews:** Semi-structured interview design, expert interviews, customer interviews
	-	Interview guide development with probing questions
	-	Recording and transcription with consent
	-	Thematic analysis and coding
	-	Minimum 12-15 interviews per segment for saturation
-	**Focus Groups:** 6-8 participants, moderated discussion
	-	Participant screening and recruitment
	-	Discussion guide with activities and exercises
	-	Observation and note-taking protocol
	-	Synthesis of themes and insights
-	**Ethnographic Research:** Contextual inquiry, observation, day-in-the-life studies
	-	Understanding users in their natural environment
	-	Identifying unarticulated needs and workarounds
	-	Photo/video documentation with analysis

### Secondary Research

-	**Industry Reports:** Gartner, Forrester, IDC, McKinsey Global Institute, CB Insights
-	**Financial Data:** SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q), annual reports, investor presentations
-	**Patent Analysis:** USPTO/WIPO searches, patent landscape mapping, technology trajectory
-	**Academic Research:** IEEE, ACM, arXiv, Google Scholar — systematic literature review
-	**Market Data:** Crunchbase, PitchBook, Statista, SimilarWeb, App Annie/data.ai
-	**News and Social:** Media monitoring, social listening, community analysis (Reddit, HN, Stack Overflow)
-	**Source Evaluation:** Recency, authority, methodology transparency, potential bias

### Quantitative Methods

-	Statistical analysis: descriptive, inferential, regression, correlation
-	Market modeling: top-down and bottom-up estimation
-	Forecasting: time series analysis, growth curve modeling, scenario planning
-	Conjoint analysis: feature preference and willingness-to-pay
-	Cluster analysis: market segmentation from behavioral data
-	Data visualization: charts, graphs, and dashboards that tell a story

### Qualitative Methods

-	Thematic analysis: coding, categorization, theme development
-	Grounded theory: building frameworks from observed data
-	Case study methodology: in-depth analysis of specific instances
-	Content analysis: systematic review of text, media, or communications
-	Triangulation: combining multiple data sources to validate findings

### Technology Radar

-	**Adopt:** Technologies proven in production, recommended for broad use
-	**Trial:** Worth pursuing in low-risk projects to build experience
-	**Assess:** Worth exploring to understand potential impact
-	**Hold:** Proceed with caution, or phase out existing use
-	Categories: languages & frameworks, platforms, tools, techniques
-	Update cadence: quarterly review with engineering team input
-	Evidence-based placement: production experience, community health, trajectory

### Proof of Concept Design

-	**Objective:** Clear success criteria, not "explore the technology"
-	**Scope:** Minimum viable evaluation addressing the highest-risk questions
-	**Duration:** Time-boxed (typically 2-4 weeks)
-	**Evaluation criteria:** Performance benchmarks, integration feasibility, developer experience
-	**Team:** Small, senior team with clear ownership
-	**Documentation:** Findings, data collected, recommendation, next steps
-	**Decision gate:** Go/no-go based on predefined criteria

### Customer Interview Guide Template

```markdown
# Customer Interview Guide — [Research Objective]

## Introduction (2 minutes)
- Thank the participant
- Explain the purpose (learning, not selling)
- Confirm recording consent
- "There are no right or wrong answers"

## Current State (10 minutes)
- Walk me through how you currently [task/workflow]
- What tools do you use today?

- How much time do you spend on this per week?

## Pain Points (10 minutes)
- What is the biggest challenge you face with [domain]?
- Can you tell me about a recent time when [problem] occurred?
- What have you tried to solve this? What worked/didn't work?
- How does this problem affect your team/business?

## Solution Exploration (10 minutes)
- If you could wave a magic wand, what would the ideal solution look like?
- [Show concept/prototype if applicable]
- What is most/least appealing about this approach?
- What would prevent you from adopting a solution like this?

## Buying Context (5 minutes)
- Who is involved in deciding to adopt new tools?
- What is your budget process for tools like this?
- What would a successful evaluation look like?

## Wrap-up (3 minutes)
- Is there anything else I should know?
- Can I follow up if I have additional questions?
- [Thank and compensate]
```

### Survey Design Best Practices

-	Start with screening questions to filter relevant respondents
-	Progress from general to specific (funnel approach)
-	Avoid leading questions, double-barreled questions, and loaded language
-	Include attention check questions for online surveys
-	Keep surveys under 10 minutes (15-20 questions maximum)
-	Pre-test with 5-10 respondents before full launch
-	Offer appropriate incentives aligned with respondent value

## Due Diligence

### Technical Due Diligence Checklist

#### Architecture and Code Quality

- [ ] Architecture review: scalability, modularity, technical debt assessment
- [ ] Code quality metrics: test coverage, static analysis results, code complexity
- [ ] Technology stack evaluation: currency, community support, licensing
- [ ] Security audit: vulnerability assessment, authentication/authorization review
- [ ] Data architecture: storage, processing, migration feasibility
- [ ] Infrastructure: hosting, deployment, monitoring, disaster recovery
- [ ] Documentation: architecture docs, API docs, runbooks, onboarding guides

#### Team Assessment

- [ ] Team size and composition: engineering, product, design, data
- [ ] Key person risk: knowledge concentration, bus factor analysis
- [ ] Hiring velocity: open positions, time-to-hire, attrition rate
- [ ] Engineering culture: development practices, code review, testing discipline
- [ ] Technical leadership: CTO/VP Eng experience, vision, execution track record

#### Product Assessment

- [ ] Product-market fit signals: retention, NPS, revenue growth, usage depth
- [ ] Competitive moat: technology differentiation, data advantage, network effects
- [ ] Scalability: current load, growth trajectory, scaling limitations
- [ ] Integration ecosystem: API quality, partner integrations, extensibility
- [ ] Intellectual property: patents, trade secrets, open-source obligations

#### Financial/Operational

- [ ] Revenue model sustainability: unit economics, margin trajectory
- [ ] Customer concentration risk: top 10 customer revenue percentage
- [ ] Infrastructure costs: hosting spend, cost per user, efficiency trends
- [ ] Compliance: regulatory requirements, certifications, data handling

### Scalability Assessment

-	Current performance benchmarks (requests/sec, latency percentiles, throughput)
-	Load testing results and failure modes
-	Horizontal scaling capability and automation
-	Database scaling strategy (read replicas, sharding, caching)
-	Cost scaling model: cost per user at 10x, 100x current scale
-	Identified bottlenecks and remediation plans

## Trend Analysis

### Technology Lifecycle Assessment

-	**Innovation Trigger:** New technology emerges, early prototypes, limited real-world use
-	**Peak of Inflated Expectations:** Early publicity, vendor hype, speculative investment
-	**Trough of Disillusionment:** Failures emerge, interest wanes, consolidation begins
-	**Slope of Enlightenment:** Practical applications mature, second/third generation products
-	**Plateau of Productivity:** Mainstream adoption, clear ROI, stable market
-	Position each relevant technology on the lifecycle curve with evidence

### Gartner Hype Cycle Positioning

-	Map technologies relevant to our domain on the hype cycle
-	Track movement over time (quarterly snapshots)
-	Identify technologies approaching the Plateau (investment opportunities)
-	Identify technologies at the Peak (caution against premature adoption)
-	Cross-reference with our technology radar

### Adoption Curve Analysis

-	**Innovators (2.5%):** Technology enthusiasts, willing to experiment
-	**Early Adopters (13.5%):** Visionaries, strategic advantage seekers
-	**Early Majority (34%):** Pragmatists, need proven solutions
-	**Late Majority (34%):** Conservatives, adopt under pressure
-	**Laggards (16%):** Skeptics, last to adopt
-	Identify where target market sits on the curve
-	Adapt messaging and product strategy for the current adoption stage
-	"Crossing the chasm" analysis: are we ready for Early Majority?
